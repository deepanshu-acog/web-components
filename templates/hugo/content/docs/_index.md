Everything you need to build reports and sites with **atk-ui** and **Web
Awesome**. Use the tree on the left to browse, or the "On This Page" panel on
the right to jump within a page.

## What's here

- **Getting Started** — install the CLI, scaffold a project, preview a report.
- **Layouts** — the standard page layouts (`dossier`, `dashboard`, `tabbed`,
  `docs`, `landing`) and when to reach for each.
- **Components** — the atk-ui catalog on top of Web Awesome.

## The two families of page

atk-ui pages fall into two families, and the chrome adapts automatically:

- **Reading views** (`dossier`, `dashboard`, `tabbed`, `docs`, `single`) get a
  left sidebar and a right "On This Page" table of contents.
- **Marketing views** (`landing`) get a full-width hero and section layout with
  no side navigation — just the top navbar.
